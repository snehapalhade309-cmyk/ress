Mathematical Formulation & Weights

ResQAI calculates a continuous risk score \(R \in [5, 100]\) to rank emergencies transparently:

R = Base(Hazard) + Sev + Pop(Civilian) + Vuln(Needs) + Env(Weather)
  • Hazard Base: Flood (35), Landslide (40), Fire (35), Earthquake (45), Severe Weather (25).
  • Reported Severity: Minor (+5), Moderate (+15), Severe (+28), Catastrophic (+40).
  • Population Scale: 0 people (+2), 1-5 (+8), 6-20 (+16), 21-50 (+24), 50+ (+30).
  • Vulnerability Multiplier: Trapped (+15), Medical Oxygen (+12), Mobility (+9), Elderly (+7), Children (+6). Capped at +25 pts.
  • Weather Amplifier: Heavy Rain \(\ge 15\text{mm/hr}\) (+10-15 pts for flood/mudslide), Gale Wind \(\ge 40\text{km/h}\) (+14 pts for fire spread).

Risk Tier Thresholds

CRITICAL TIER (Score 78.0 – 100.0)
Immediate life-safety peril, rapid water inundation, mass trapped populations. Auto-generates critical alerts.
HIGH TIER (Score 55.0 – 77.9)
Major property loss, arterial road cutoffs, vulnerable cluster present. Priority resource dispatch recommended.
MEDIUM TIER (Score 30.0 – 54.9)
Moderate disruptions, isolated flooding, localized collisions. Standard queue routing.
LOW TIER (Score 5.0 – 29.9)
Minor hazards, advisory notices, standby surveillance without immediate life danger.

About ResQAI

Mission architecture, heuristic risk model, API strategy, and system disclosures.

System Mission & Capabilities

ResQAI is an intelligent emergency management and climate response decision-support system built for localized disaster triage, rapid situational awareness, and resource dispatch coordination.

Designed with a local-first architecture, ResQAI operates without mandatory API keys, using public OpenStreetMap tiles, Open-Meteo meteorological telemetry, Nominatim public geocoding, and OSRM routing with offline geodesic fallbacks.

Core Operational Disclosures:

  • Decision-Support Heuristic: The AI Risk Engine uses a deterministic, transparent multi-factor scoring model. It does not replace official command decisions.
  • Simulated Fleet Units: Response units (ambulances, boats, fire trucks) are simulated for operational decision support and training.
  • Advisory Evacuation Routes: Evacuation paths computed via OSRM or geodesic interpolation do not confirm real-time road flooding or physical passability. Physical reconnaissance is mandatory.
